COMPATIBILITY
WebXR requires HTTPS and a compatible immersive-vr runtime. PICO G2 hardware
has not been tested. Native APK is a monoscopic Android development build:
no proprietary PICO compositor, lens correction or controller-pose SDK.
Single-controller direction is used in WebXR; no two-hand or roomscale requirement.
